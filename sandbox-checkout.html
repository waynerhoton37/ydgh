Got Questions?

Questions people actually ask

What does the website itself cost?

Nothing for the build! The custom website design is completely free when you start your subscription upfront.

What does the $99/month plan cover?

The $99/month plan includes website design, hosting, one standard domain costing up to $20/year, one basic email address, routine backups, basic security upkeep, and weekly website updates. Premium domains, additional mailboxes, or advanced services may require a custom quote.

Am I locked into a contract?

Never. Our service is completely month-to-month. We believe in keeping you through great service, not contracts.

What happens to my site if I cancel?

You own your domain, content, and site files. We manage the domain for you and will help transfer control to you whenever you request it, for any reason. Any fees charged by a receiving registrar are separate.

How long does the build take?

Typically around 1 to 2 weeks from our initial chat to your staging link review.
